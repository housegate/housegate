# Plan S3-E4/S3-F decision D45: devnet2's shared failure domain (D41) and census rule (D44) in Plans S3-E4 and S3-F (user decisions, 2026-11-02)

Plan S3-D's decision D41 (`2026-10-29-s3d-d41-devnet2-shared-node.md`) keeps devnet2's SI ClickHouses where Task 53 found them. indexer-a's and indexer-b's sources and verifier-1..3 run on sea-dg-7, and indexer-c's source runs on sea-dg-3. The ledger records `DEVNET2_FAILURE_DOMAINS=0=node:sea-dg-7,1=node:sea-dg-7,<INDEXER_C_ID>=node:sea-dg-3` (`INDEXER_C_ID` = 2), so devnet2's failure domains are not independent. D41 names two downstream blockers and asks for a bounded amendment before Plans S3-E4 and S3-F run:

- S3-E4's `si_p5_py cmd_params` refuses any repeated `failure_domain`.
- S3-F's `daily_copies` requires three distinct source nodes.

Its bounded rule is: a repeated domain is accepted only for exactly the indexers D41 records on one node; any other repetition is still refused; copies and `min_safe_copies` are counted per distinct failure domain, never per indexer; the P5 activation record and the P6 daily-copies report state the non-independence.

Decision D44 (`2026-11-01-s3d-d44-devnet2-d42-backup-check-exception.md`) binds later plans: a policy they introduce or tighten needs a census covering every client's period, or a fresh user decision, and a functional check they adopt takes its drop read in the same run.

## What D41 does to the two plans

A read-only impact analysis of 2026-11-02 against housegate `origin/main` `f987cbb`, arbiter `urwt/claude/multi-source-s3e` `6a6653e` and arbiter-core `v0.21.0` found more than the two named blockers. Plan S3-F assumes two members per group throughout, and D41 makes that impossible on devnet2:

- PlaceGroup (`arbiter:fsm/placement.go:120-170`) skips a candidate whose domain is the owner's or an already chosen member's (`"failure_domain"`). Every member's `failure_domain` equals its host's `indexer_failure_domains` entry (`fsm/stage3.go:322-323`, K68), so member-a and member-b are both `node:sea-dg-7`.
- Owner a's and owner b's groups are therefore `[member-c]` for every database. Owner c's group is `[member-a]` or `[member-b]` per database, by the per-database salt.
- `RebalanceGroup` refuses the shared-domain peer (`rebalance group: %s is in the owner's failure domain %q`, `rebalance group: %s and %s share failure domain %q`), so no forward fix reaches two members per group.
- The copy floor (`fsm/stage3_groups.go:413-439`) and every copy count already count distinct domains other than the owner's, so D41's "never per indexer" already holds in code. Plan S3-E4 Task 76 already tests a collided domain counting once.
- Every group's verifier floor passes: 1 verify member plus the 3 pool verifiers (`network_fill` forced on).
- k = 2 is met on two real nodes for every owner, but with no margin: `safe_copies` is exactly 2, the owner and its group's one member.

No arbiter, arbiter-core, sentio-node or housegate code changes. The changes are plan text and the plans' local runbook helpers, fixtures and records.

## What the si-member does with copies outside its groups

This was read in arbiter-core `v0.21.0` (`ef051e2`), the `CORE_TAG_P5` member that Plan S3-F runs. A member in consensus mode keeps two kinds of local state: the P2/P3 `hg_replica` copies and the P6 `hg_member_unsafe` replicas. They are scoped differently.

**The `hg_replica` copies are scoped by the configured peers, never by the groups.**
- The role builds the copyset, the installer and their sources from `Config.Peers`, the static P2 scope: `member/role.go:174-176` and `:376-397`. The copyset reconciler keeps one copy of every Pending or Active incarnation of every owner in that scope and drops a copy only from Retiring on, or when its owner leaves the scope: `member/copyset/reconciler.go:1-10` and `:45-47`.
- The consensus mode is handed the same list as its `Owners`: `member/consensus_mode.go:48-55`.
- So after placement member-a keeps installing owner b's databases from verified manifests and auditing them, member-b does the same for owner a's, and the unchosen member does so for owner c's. Nothing drops them.

**Such a copy is still served on its own host, under P3's gates only.**
- The serving view lists every database of every configured peer that has installed copies: `member/serving.go:44-49` and `:153-174`.
- It serves one only while the owner is quarantined or unreachable, the owner's published entry is confirmed over two polls, the audit is clean and the copy is at that entry: `member/serve/conditions.go:236-263`. No condition reads a group.
- The host's table state then serves the database read-only (`housegate:pkg/sitable/sitable.go:222-237`, `ServedDatabases`).
- No failover read is routed to such a copy. From placement on, `replica_hosts` list only the hosts of the group's members (S3-E4 §56, Task 79's `ReplicaHostsFromGroups`). A copy outside the groups is reached only by a client already connected to that host while the owner is unavailable.
- The guarantees of such a read are P3's: a manifest-verified, audited copy at the owner's published entry. Placement takes nothing away from that verification. It only stops counting the copy.

**Such a copy is never checkpointed and never counts.**
- `PlanCheckpoint` answers false for an owner none of whose groups lists the node: `member/consensus/checkpoint.go:78-124`. `checkpointStep` then skips that owner: `member/consensus/mode.go:373-394`. Its checkpoint series still exist at 0, declared for every configured owner (`member/consensus/metrics.go:88-97`), and are never advanced.
- The arbiter's E, the floor, `arbiter_safe_copies_min` and S3-F's `si_safe_copies` count only current group members with a current checkpoint (`arbiter:fsm/stage3_groups.go:413-439`; S3-F K87(b)).

**No `hg_member_unsafe` replica exists outside the groups, and none is attested.**
- The reconciler creates a replica only while the member is placed in the incarnation's group, and drops one that is no longer placed once its table is quiet: `member/consensus/unsafe_reconciler.go:146-213` and `:253-271`. Replicas first appear at P6 step 6, after placement, so none is ever created outside a group.
- The verify role refuses to attest a block naming a table it holds no ready replica of (`ErrMemberTableNotReady`): `member/consensus/verify_role.go:291-328`. It is in no eligible ring outside its groups in any case.
- A Purging incarnation the member never held is reported purged at the first step that sees it: `member/consensus/purge.go:82-131`. A member outside a group never delays a purge.

**A member listed in no group starts and runs normally.**
- At startup step (6) the governed-host check walks only the groups that name the member: `member/role.go:264-270` and `member/consensus_mode.go:97-108`. No group means nothing to check, and startup does not fail.
- It registers as a VERIFIER as every member does (`mode.go:274-295`).
- Readiness has nothing to wait for. `awaitingReadiness` and `ReplicaPaths` list only its groups (`member/consensus/readiness.go:49-100`), and `readinessStep` returns at once on no paths (`mode.go:321-333`).
- The serving view's `consensus.groups` is `[]` (`member/consensus/status.go:46-63`), and `sentio_node_si_member_ready_groups` is 0 (`status.go:65-75`).
- Its `hg_member_unsafe` replica list and its `ready-<x>.jsonl` are empty.

**Verdict.** Nothing here is unsafe. One point differed from the user's first wording ("unread"): such a copy is still served on its own host while its owner is unavailable. The user accepted that in chat (「可以接受，写进 D45」, decision 2 above). The decision is therefore: the copies outside the groups stay installed and verified, are never checkpointed or counted, are not in `replica_hosts`, and are served on their own host only under P3's owner-unavailable conditions (`member/serve/conditions.go:236-263`). The plans record this. Whether to drop these copies is left to the amendment that lifts D41.

## Decision D45

**D45: Plans S3-E4 and S3-F on devnet2 under D41 and D44.**

- **Failure domains (D41).** `DEVNET2_FAILURE_DOMAINS` may repeat a domain only as D41 records it: indexers 0 and 1 on `node:sea-dg-7`. Every other repetition is refused, by S3-E4's `si_p5_py params` and record tool and by S3-F's source-node checks. Copies, E and `min_safe_copies` are counted per distinct failure domain, never per indexer, as the arbiter already counts them.
- **Statements.** The P5 activation record, its ledger steady state and the runbook's parameter output state D41's non-independence in D41's words: "D41: devnet2's failure domains are not independent (indexer-a and indexer-b share node:sea-dg-7 with every verifier ClickHouse), by the user's decision of 2026-10-29". So do S3-F's daily observations, P6 run record and ledger record.
- **Groups.** On devnet2 every group holds one member: owners a and b `[member-c]`, owner c `[member-a]` or `[member-b]` per database. `safe_copies` is exactly 2 on two distinct nodes. k = 2 is accepted with that shape (user decision 1):
  - one member down holds its groups' cleanups until it is Lagging or the pressure bypass applies, and `under_replicated` is raised;
  - a member-c outage holds owners a and b together;
  - member restarts run strictly one at a time, and each restart window counts against K87's 1800 s bound;
  - an outage over 30 minutes breaches the bound and restarts the 7 days.
- **Anti-affinity.** S3-F's `si_antiaffinity` applies the predicate of Plan S3-D Task 55 Step 20. The ClickHouses `DEVNET2_SHARED_NODE_EXCEPTION` records, on their recorded node, are printed as one `verdict SHARE D41 sea-dg-7: …` line. A recorded one elsewhere stops, and every other row keeps D39.
- **`replica_hosts`.** From step 5 each database lists the served hosts of its groups' current members: one host per database. Step 5 compares the new lists with the placed groups through `si_p6_py replica-hosts-placed`, and `replica-hosts-before-step5.jsonl` stays as evidence. Read failover for owners a and b rests on indexer-c alone. `MemberReady` still changes no list.
- **Copies outside the groups (user decision 2 and its confirmation).** They stay installed and verified, are never checkpointed or counted, are not listed in `replica_hosts`, and are served on their own host only under P3's owner-unavailable conditions (`member/serve/conditions.go:236-263`); the plans record this. A member in no group passes the step-6 checks vacuously: no replica, no readiness and an empty `ready-<x>.jsonl`.
- **Rings and pairs.** Rings, checkpoint pairs and counter checks are derived from the groups, not from "every other host's member":
  - a ready ring is the pool plus the group's ready member: 4 ids;
  - the checked (member, owner) pairs are those of the groups: 3, or 4 if owner c's databases split;
  - each table's owner-side replica count is 4, then 5 once its group's member runs, never 6.
- **D44.** Neither plan introduces or tightens a network policy or the Keeper allowlist. A later step that does needs a census covering every client's period, or a fresh user decision; D42's window and D44's acceptance are never reused. S3-F takes a same-run `si_cnp_drops` read around each D-x CHI apply and each host's step-6 hour, and a dropped flow stops for a user decision.
- **Bounds.** D45 is limited to devnet2 under D41. It is lifted with D41 by a later amendment, for example dedicated nodes, and is never widened by an edit.
